About QCTO PathFinder and smarter learnership searching

About the platform and creator

A learner-facing tool that helps users search with strategy, not just apply and hope.

Learnership selection is not only luck. It is also positioning. The platform helps users understand the field, qualification, provider, timing and message before they apply.

What is QCTO?

The Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO) is the quality assurance body for occupational qualifications, trades and skills development in South Africa.

QCTO works with partners to keep occupational training aligned to national standards, assessment requirements and labour-market needs.

Visit the QCTO website ↗

About the data

The information on this platform is sourced from official QCTO public datasets, including accredited Skills Development Providers, qualifications and Quality Partners.

Because one provider can hold multiple accreditations, the platform treats the data as accreditation records rather than a simple list of unique organisations.

View QCTO data source ↗

Why this platform was created

Finding the right qualification, SETA-linked pathway or provider should not feel impossible.

This platform was created to help learners, job seekers and career changers move from a broad interest to relevant qualification pathways, accredited provider records and practical enquiry steps.

How SETAs fit into the system

SETAs are sector skills authorities that connect employer needs, skills levies, grants and workplace learning.

QCTO accreditation is quality assurance. SETA funding and current intakes are separate, so users must verify opportunities directly with providers.

PathFinder brings these parts together into a clearer search journey.
